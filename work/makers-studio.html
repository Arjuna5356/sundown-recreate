MAKERS STUDIO HOI

Discipline

Spatial & Fabrication

Category

Environments

Year

2023

Scope

End-to-End Build

Creating an environment where creative prototyping meets architectural discipline.

Makers Studio HOI was conceived as a versatile, modular laboratory for creative makers, industrial designers, and digital artists. Sundown engineered custom workstations, integrated modular acoustic baffles, and fabricated bespoke display fixtures that allow the space to transform between collaborative workshops and exhibition showcases seamlessly.

Materiality & Tactile Craftsmanship

Every element was manufactured with natural materials, brushed aluminum framing, and warm ambient illumination. The environment reflects the studio philosophy that physical details elevate the entire experience.
